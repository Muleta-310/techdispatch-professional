Our Story

Built in Alaska.
Trusted Across Alaska.

Founded in 1986, Tech Dispatch began as a small computer repair shop in Anchorage. Over four decades we have grown into the region's premier full-service IT company — but our roots remain firmly Alaskan.

We understand the unique challenges that come with operating in Alaska: extreme weather, remote locations, and limited vendor access. That's why we build relationships, not just service tickets.
